A block theme built to be edited by people and by AI agents.

== Description ==

Every colour, font size, space, radius and shadow is a named token in theme.json, so a page changes with the site's palette and fonts instead of keeping old values. Sections take their colours from section styles (Tint, Brand soft, Brand, Dark, Second brand, Card). Every pattern says what it is for, which helps both the pattern inserter and AI agents pick the right one.

Style variations: six colour palettes (Forest, Plum, Ocean, Charcoal and gold, Rose, Night) and four font pairings (Warm serif, Editorial, Sturdy, Friendly), in Appearance > Editor > Styles. Every palette keeps text at WCAG AA contrast or better in every section style.

Starter pages: home pages for trades and local services, food and drink, and classes and memberships, plus About, Services and Contact pages, a long sales page, an email signup page and a thank-you page. A links page (for the link in a social media bio) is there too. They appear when a new page is created.

Kits: thirteen sets designed for one kind of business each. A kit is a style variation, its own sections and a complete home page:

* Ridge, for trades and home services: condensed capitals, hi-vis yellow, flat colour panels and the phone number as the main action.
* Hearth, for cafés, bakeries and restaurants: a printed menu card with dotted lines to each price, olive and mustard.
* Still, for spas, therapists and studios: light serif type, full-screen photography and plenty of space.
* Ledger, for accountants, lawyers, consultants and agencies: a report layout, a strong sans for titles and deep teal.
* Parcel, for online shops and makers: a product photo beside the collection, a narrow serif for names, clay and cream.
* Chapter, for online courses, coaching and memberships: school yellow and ink blue, a curriculum that opens week by week.
* Gloss, for salons, barbers and beauty studios: a high-contrast serif, blush and oxblood, the price list with dotted lines.
* Commons, for chambers of commerce, associations, clubs and churches: events with date blocks, membership levels, navy and brick.
* Rep, for gyms and fitness studios: heavy italic capitals, acid lime, the weekly timetable as a table.
* Frame, for photographers, designers and architects: one big sentence, staggered work with numbered captions, black and cobalt.
* Keystone, for estate agents and realtors: listings with price and facts, a featured home, forest green and brass.
* Launch, for software products and startups: one grotesk at every size, features without icons, pricing and questions.
* Byline, for blogs, magazines and newsletters: a masthead between rules, the newest post large and the rest in a grid.

Apply the kit's style in Appearance > Editor > Styles, then start a page from the kit's home page. The kit's sections are in the inserter under "Kit: ...".

AnyPress Blocks: 32 shared sections any site can use, whatever its kit, in eight groups in the inserter (Heroes, About, Services and prices, Proof, Calls to action, Contact, Blog, Footers). They take the site's colours and fonts, so they match the kit in use.

Post outlines: starting structures for a how-to guide, a list post and a product review, in the inserter under "Post outlines" when writing a post.
